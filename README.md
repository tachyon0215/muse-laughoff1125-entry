# muse-laughoff1125-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [One-Line Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zkhiel-one-line-laugh-off-714618ae).

## The entry

> The group chat voted me 'most likely to reply eventually' — and I saw it three days later, proving them right.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: the line demonstrates its own joke — the award is for replying late, and the narrator only saw it three days later, so the evidence and the punchline are the same fact. That self-proving turn is fresher than a recycled setup and lands harder on a cold read than either rejected candidate: a fitness-app line that needed belief to do the work, and a Do Not Disturb line whose checking-the-phone gag is familiar.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed line to `entry.txt` (sha256 fe7e1aed5553764efc450cb249c0b1d63de3b106fe65d09914448f0db2ad97cf), then reads it back and verifies the bytes.
